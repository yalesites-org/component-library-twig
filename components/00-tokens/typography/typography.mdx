# Typography

The YaleSites type system is built around Yale's brand typefaces and a structured scale of sizes, weights, and styles. Use the **Typography: Heading Font**, **Typography: Heading Numerals**, and **Typography: Body Numerals** toolbar controls to preview components in any combination.

## Heading Font Pairings

YaleSites gives site owners three independent typography choices instead of one bundled pairing. The body font (Mallory) is fixed and does not vary by site.

- **Heading font** — Yale New (the default) or Mallory.
- **Heading numeral style** — Old-Style Numerals (the default) or Lining Numerals. Applies to numbers in headings, independent of which heading font is chosen.
- **Body numeral style** — Old-Style Numerals (the default) or Lining Numerals. Applies to numbers in body text, independent of the heading numeral style.

Old-Style Numerals vary in height, with some digits sitting below the baseline — Yale's brand standard, recommended for most sites. Lining Numerals sit uniformly at cap height, which suits data, tables, or financial figures better.
