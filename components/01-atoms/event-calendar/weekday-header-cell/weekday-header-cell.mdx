## Technical Specifications

- **Atomic Level**: Atom

### Accessibility

- The hidden label holds the full weekday; the short text is `aria-hidden`.
- Gray 800 on white is well above 4.5:1.
- Size, weight and color are not in the spec; they follow the Date badge weekday. Confirm against the design.
