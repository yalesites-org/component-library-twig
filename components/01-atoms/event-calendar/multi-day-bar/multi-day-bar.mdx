# Multi-day bar

A 24px link bar that spans a multi-day event's days within a week in the Full month grid, under the day numbers. It takes one event slot in each day it covers and stays light so it never outranks single events.

## Technical Specifications

- **Atomic Level**: Atom
- **Template**: `yds-multi-day-bar.twig`
- **Styles**: `_yds-multi-day-bar.scss`
- **Rules**: tokens only, no color props, states are props, the parent sets outer margin and width

### Accessibility

- A link. Its accessible name starts "Multi-day event, October 22 to 24:" then the title; the visible " | " and date range are hidden from assistive tech to avoid repeating it.
- Past colors are not in the spec: Gray 600 title, Gray 100 fill, Gray 500 border. Confirm against the design.
