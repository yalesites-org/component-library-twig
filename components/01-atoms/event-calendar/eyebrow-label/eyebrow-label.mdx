### Accessibility

- Plain text; the label names today, a past event, a multi-day event or the category.
- Gray 600 on white is 6.48:1. Today is Gray 800, darker still.
- Size, weight and color are not in the spec; they follow the Date badge weekday. Confirm against the design.
