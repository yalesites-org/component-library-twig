# Date badge

A badge that groups list items by day: the weekday in small caps above the date number, or a date range. Used in Compact month with list and the phone day list.

## Technical Specifications

- **Atomic Level**: Atom
- **Template**: `yds-date-badge.twig`
- **Styles**: `_yds-date-badge.scss`
- **Rules**: tokens only, no color props, states are props, the parent sets outer margin and width

### Accessibility

- The full date is hidden text; the visible weekday and date are hidden from assistive tech.
- Gray 600 fill with white text is 6.5:1; Yale Blue on Blue Soft clears 4.5:1.
- Badge size is not in the spec: a 3.75rem square minimum, read from the design.
