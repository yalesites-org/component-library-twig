# Byline

A simple meta component for displaying author information and publication dates. Commonly used in articles, news posts, and blog entries. On a post it sits under the heading in the [Page Header](?path=/docs/molecules-page-header--overview).

## When to use it

Use the Byline when the content is a standard editorial piece — articles, news items, or blog-style entries — where the reader needs to know who wrote it and when it was published.

Don't use the Byline when the content is an event, profile, or resource. Those types have dedicated meta components with fields specific to their content shape.
